01The wall

Every watt starts at the socket.

A metering smart plug — Shelly, Tasmota or Kasa — reads true wall power over your Wi-Fi. No plug? WattWatch estimates it, and tells you which one you're looking at.

02The cable

Then it flows into the machine.

Each pulse on the cable is energy you pay for. The pulses speed up as the load climbs. Try the load buttons in the corner.

03Inside

Every component, every second.

CPU from RAPL or powermetrics, GPU from NVML, and a model for memory, storage, fans and the board, sampled once a second.

04The power supply

Some of it never arrives.

AC to DC conversion turns 10–28% of the energy into heat before it reaches a component. A big PSU idling at low load is at its worst. WattWatch shows you exactly how much.

05The orb

This is what 72 watts looks like.

The orb's colour, pulse and storm follow your real wattage. Push the load and watch the room react: fans spin up, pulses race, the plasma turns hot.

06The bill

At $0.17/kWh. WattWatch shows what you spend today, this month and per year, what's wasted, and how much sleeping at night would save. It's free, open source, and your data never leaves your machine.
